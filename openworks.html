How the month runs

Four days. Then it is yours.

Day 1

Sit and watch

I shadow the actual work — the inbox, the counter, the phone, the spreadsheet nobody wants to open. We pick one job worth fixing, not five.

Day 2

Build the ugly version

Working tool, same afternoon, on your real data. It goes in front of your team while it is still rough, because that is when the gaps show up.

Day 3

Make it theirs

Rename the fields, match the language your staff already use, kill the parts they will never touch. Then they drive while I watch.

Day 4

Hand over the keys

Written runbook, a short recording, and a standing invite to the monthly workshop. If I disappear, it keeps working.

Straight answers

Questions owners ask.

Is this really free?

Yes. OpenWorks funds the partner, so the business pays nothing for the hands-on help, and the three months of ChatGPT Business come from the program. I do not invoice you for program work. If you later want something outside the program — a full site, custom software — that is a normal paid project and we would scope it separately, in writing.

Do I have to be technical?

No. That is the whole point. You bring the business problem; I bring the build and the training. The goal is that your team runs it without me by the second month.

Are you officially OpenAI's Houston partner?

Not yet — I applied on Nov 25, 2026, the week the program opened, and Houston is one of the 20 pilot cities. OpenAI reviews applications and matches partners to businesses. What I can do today is the same work, on the same terms, while that review runs. If OpenAI matches us through the program, even better.

What happens to my data?

Your data stays in your accounts wherever possible — the point is to build on the tools you already own, not to copy your business into someone else's system. Anything touched during a session is listed in writing before we start, and nothing is used to train a model.

How much time will this take from me?

Plan on a half day for the first session and a couple of hours of feedback after. If your week cannot spare that, the project will not stick, and I would rather tell you now.

I am outside Houston. Can you still help?

The program is city-based, so the free in-person work is Houston only. Remote projects are a normal BookiStudio engagement — bookistudios.com.
